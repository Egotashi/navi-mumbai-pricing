About the data

  • Source: Makaan.com flat listings scraped in 2024 and published as a public Kaggle dataset (Apache-2.0). Listings carry no posting date, so the data is a 2024 snapshot rather than a monthly series. Repeat listings of the same flat were removed on node, BHK, area and price.
  • "Listed area" is whatever the portal reported, which for many projects is built-up or super built-up area rather than RERA carpet area. Compare rates within this dataset, not against carpet-area quotes.
  • The data has no unit floor level, possession status, sale dates, absorption or lead data, so the dashboard covers pricing only.
