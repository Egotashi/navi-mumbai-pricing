About the data

  • Three public datasets were combined: 2024 Makaan listings (area, furnishing, age, coordinates), an MMR Makaan scrape (possession status) and a listings set with 13 amenity flags. Duplicates were removed across and within sources on node, BHK, area and price.
  • "Listed area" is whatever the portal reported, which for many projects is built-up or super built-up area rather than RERA carpet area. Compare rates within this dataset, not against carpet-area quotes.
  • The data has no unit floor level, sale dates, absorption or lead data, so the dashboard covers pricing and supply mix only.
